Discover the ultimate in luxury and capability with this 2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee L Limited. Boasting a sleek white exterior and a powerful 3.6L V6 24V VVT engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, this SUV is ready for any adventure with its 4WD capabilities.
