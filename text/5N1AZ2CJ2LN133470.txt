## Dealer Notes
The 2020 Nissan Murano SL is a sophisticated and capable midsize crossover delivering a premium driving experience. Powered by a 3.5L V6 with Xtronic CVT, it offers smooth, responsive, and efficient performance. The interior features heated front and rear seats, a heated steering wheel, leather-appointed seating, and NissanConnect infotainment with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. The spacious cargo area includes 60/40 split-folding rear seats. Safety features include Blind Spot Warning, Rear Automatic Braking, Intelligent Around View Monitor, Automatic Emergency Braking, and Intelligent Forward Collision Warning.
