## Dealer Notes
The 2025 Jeep Wrangler Sahara blends rugged off-road capability with advanced technology and refined comfort. Powered by a 3.6L V6 engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and a legendary 4-wheel drive system, it is ready for any terrain. The premium interior features heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, 12.3" touchscreen, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto. Safety features include Electronic Stability Control, Traction Control, and a comprehensive airbag system. The ParkView Rear Back-Up Camera and Integrated Off-Road Camera enhance situational awareness.
