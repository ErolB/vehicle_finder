## Dealer Notes
The 2025 Jeep Wrangler Rubicon X combines off-road capability with premium comfort and advanced technology. Powered by a 3.6L V6 with an 8-speed automatic transmission, it achieves 18 city/23 highway MPG. Features include a 4.56 rear axle ratio, 35-inch off-road tires, heated steering wheel, heated front seats, 12.3" touchscreen with navigation, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, Blind Spot Detection, ParkSense Rear Park Assist, and Jeep Trail Rated kit. The exterior features a body-color hardtop, steel bumpers, and Rubicon branding.
