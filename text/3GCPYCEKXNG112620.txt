## Dealer Notes

Discover the ultimate off-road companion in the 2022 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 LTD Custom Trail Boss. This rugged and capable pickup is ready to conquer any terrain, whether you're tackling the toughest trails or navigating the daily commute.

Key highlights include:
- Chevytec spray-on bedliner with Chevrolet logo
- Integrated trailer brake controller
- 120-volt power outlets in the bed and instrument panel
- Safety Confidence Package with advanced driver assistance technologies

Under the hood, the 2.7L Turbo engine and 8-speed automatic transmission deliver impressive power and efficiency, with an EPA-estimated 18 mpg on the highway. The four-wheel-drive system and off-road suspension with a 2-inch lift ensure you'll have the capability to go wherever your adventures take you.

Inside, the Custom Trail Boss offers a comfortable and well-appointed cabin, with features like 10-way power driver's seat with lumbar support, dual-zone climate control, and a premium audio system. The Chevrolet Infotainment 3 system with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto integration keeps you connected and entertained on the road.

Safety is also a top priority, with Forward Collision Alert, Automatic Emergency Braking, and Front Pedestrian Braking to help keep you and your passengers secure.
