Discover the thrilling performance and refined style of the 2026 Dodge Charger R/T. This captivating sports sedan blends iconic American muscle with advanced technology, delivering an unparalleled driving experience.

Powered by a potent 3.0L I6 Turbocharged engine delivering 420 horsepower, the Charger R/T achieves up to 26 MPG on the highway. Paired with an advanced 8-speed automatic transmission and all-wheel drive, this dynamic sedan provides exhilarating acceleration and confident handling in any conditions.

The bold exterior showcases a distinctive Fratzog dual stripe design with red accents, complemented by dark exterior badging and dual rear exhaust tips. The driver-focused cockpit features high-quality materials and advanced technology. With seating for up to five and ample cargo space, the Charger R/T delivers both performance and practicality.
