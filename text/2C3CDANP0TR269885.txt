## Dealer Notes
The 2026 Dodge Charger R/T is a high-performance sedan blending power, capability, and sophistication. The 3.0L I6 turbocharged engine produces 420 horsepower paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission. All-wheel drive provides confident handling in all weather conditions, while the sport-tuned suspension and large brakes deliver exceptional control.

Key features include wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, a 12.3" touchscreen with Uconnect 5, dual-zone automatic climate control, heated exterior mirrors, the Blacktop appearance package, sport steering wheel, and cloth sport seats. Advanced safety technologies include forward collision warning, automatic emergency braking, and lane departure warning.
