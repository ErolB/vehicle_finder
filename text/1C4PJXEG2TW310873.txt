The 2026 Jeep Wrangler Sahara is the sweet spot of the Wrangler lineup — rugged capability with everyday comfort — and this one turns heads in the eye-catching Goldilocks color. Under the hood is the 3.6-liter Pentastar V6 with an eight-speed automatic and Jeep's trusted 4WD, and a body-color three-piece hard top means you can drop the panels for open-air cruising on those gorgeous North Florida days. Inside, McKinley-trimmed seats, a heated steering wheel, and front dual-zone automatic climate control keep things comfortable, while the 12.3-inch Uconnect 5 touchscreen with navigation, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto handles the tech. You'll also enjoy a premium Alpine audio system with SiriusXM 360L and an auxiliary switch group ready for your accessories. The Trailer Tow group adds versatility for hauling your boat or trailer, and Blind Spot and Cross-Path Detection plus an integrated off-road camera add confidence on road and off. Whether it's the beach, the boat ramp, or the trail, this Sahara is ready to roll. Come see this standout Wrangler Sahara and take it for a spin at Beck CDJR in Palatka.
