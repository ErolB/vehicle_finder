## Dealer Notes
Take the desert-rated Gladiator and turn it up a notch — that's the 2026 Jeep Gladiator Mojave X, here in bold Black Clearcoat. This top-tier Mojave pairs the 3.6-liter Pentastar V6 with an eight-speed automatic and a tough 4WD setup, riding on a heavy-duty suspension with gas-charged shocks designed to soak up rough terrain at speed. A body-color three-piece hard top lets you go open-air whenever the mood strikes, and a MOPAR spray-in bedliner keeps the box ready for work and play. Inside, black leather seating and a leather-wrapped shift knob give it a premium feel, while the 12.3-inch Uconnect 5 touchscreen with navigation, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto keeps everything at your fingertips. A premium Alpine audio system with SiriusXM 360L, a heated steering wheel, an integrated off-road camera, and Blind Spot and Cross-Path Detection round out a seriously well-equipped truck. From the sand to the trails to the daily grind around Palatka, the Mojave X is built to handle whatever Florida throws at it.
