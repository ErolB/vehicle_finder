## Dealer Notes
Step into full-size luxury with the 2026 Jeep Grand Wagoneer, finished in elegant Bright White Clearcoat over black leather. At its heart is Jeep's advanced 3.0-liter Hurricane twin-turbo inline-six paired with an eight-speed automatic and 4WD, delivering effortless power for the whole family. This three-row flagship seats your crew in comfort, with a power-reclining 60/40 third-row seat, heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, and tri-zone automatic climate control. The 12-inch Uconnect 5 touchscreen with navigation, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto headlines the tech, alongside SiriusXM 360L and a memory driver's seat. Confidence comes standard with a Surround-View Camera System, Side Distance Warning, and Park and Unpark Assist. A four-wheel independent, auto-leveling suspension keeps the ride smooth and composed, while a garage door transmitter and luxury floor mats add everyday convenience.
