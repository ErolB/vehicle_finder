## Dealer Notes

When the trail gets serious, the 2026 Jeep Wrangler Rubicon is the one you want, finished in sharp Black Clearcoat with black leather seating. The legendary Rubicon is the most trail-capable Wrangler in the lineup, powered by the 3.6-liter Pentastar V6 and an eight-speed automatic with the 4WD muscle Jeep is famous for. This build steps things up with a 35-inch tire suspension setup, a steel bumper group, and a steel performance hood for a tougher, more aggressive stance. Inside, the 12.3-inch Uconnect 5 touchscreen with navigation, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto anchors a modern cabin, and the auxiliary switch bank up top makes adding lights or off-road gear a breeze. You'll also get a 4G LTE Wi-Fi hot spot, an auto-dimming rearview mirror, and Blind Spot and Cross-Path Detection for added confidence. Whether you're crawling through the woods, cruising A1A with the top down, or splashing through a North Florida mud hole, the Rubicon was made for it.
