Rugged, capable, and ready for adventure, the 2026 Jeep Gladiator Mojave is the ultimate off-road companion. Equipped with a powerful 3.6L V6 24V VVT engine and 8-speed automatic transmission, this Gladiator delivers impressive 4WD performance with an EPA-estimated 17 city/22 highway MPG.

Designed to conquer the toughest terrain, the Gladiator Mojave features the Uconnect 5 Navigation system with a massive 12.3" touchscreen display, along with cutting-edge connectivity options like Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, and a 4G LTE Wi-Fi hotspot. Advanced safety technologies include Blind Spot and Cross Path Detection, ParkSense Rear Park Assist, and an Emergency Communication System. The rugged exterior is complemented by a body-color 3-piece hard top and dark gray painted 17" alloy wheels.
