## Dealer Notes
The 2026 Jeep Grand Wagoneer Base blends cutting-edge technology, luxury, and off-road capability. Features include heated and ventilated front seats, dual-zone automatic climate control, Uconnect 5 with 12" display, Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, navigation, and advanced safety systems including Automatic Emergency Braking, Lane Departure Warning, and Blind Spot Monitoring.
