The Mojave package brings serious desert-rated hardware, finished with a body-color three-piece hard top. Inside, black leather seating sets the tone, and the 12.3-inch Uconnect 5 touchscreen with navigation, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto keeps you connected. Driver-confidence features include Blind Spot and Cross-Path Detection and an integrated off-road camera.
