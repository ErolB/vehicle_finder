# 2026 Ram 2500 Big Horn Crew Cab 4x4

Powered by a 430-hp 6.7L Cummins HO Turbo Diesel producing 1,075 lb-ft of torque, this heavy-duty pickup tows up to 19,970 lbs. Features include a 12" Uconnect touchscreen, Alpine audio, heated seats, MOPAR spray-in bedliner, 4G LTE Wi-Fi, Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, and 4x4 capability in striking Diamond Black Crystal Pearlcoat.
