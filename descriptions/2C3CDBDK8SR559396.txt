The 2025 Dodge Charger Daytona Scat Pack AWD is an all-electric muscle car delivering 630 hp, 627 lb.-ft. torque, and 241 miles of range. Features include AWD, Track Pack with widebody suspension, adaptive damping, heated/ventilated seats, 12.3" Uconnect navigation, and Apple CarPlay. Priced at $66,114.
